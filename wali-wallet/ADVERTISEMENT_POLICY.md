# wAli Advertisement Policy

**Helpful ads, privacy-first, user-friendly 🦭**

## Our Commitment

wAli shows advertisements to support sustainable development while keeping the wallet **friendly, fast, and privacy-respecting**. This policy explains what we allow, what we prohibit, and how we protect users.

## Core Principles

### 1. **User Experience First**
- Ads must not interrupt critical wallet operations
- No pop-ups, auto-play videos, or intrusive formats
- All ads can be dismissed/closed
- Frequency limits enforced (max 5/session, 20/day)

### 2. **Privacy-Respecting**
- Respect "Do Not Track" browser setting
- Opt-in only for any user tracking
- No personal data collection without consent
- Contextual targeting only (based on action, not identity)

### 3. **Transparency**
- Clear "Sponsored" labels on all ads
- Distinguish ads from organic content
- No disguised or misleading placements

### 4. **Quality & Safety**
- Ads reviewed before approval
- Scams, phishing, and malware prohibited
- Claims must be substantiated
- Family-friendly content only

## Ad Placements

### Allowed Placements

#### ✅ **Transaction Confirmation Screens**
- **When**: After transaction is confirmed (not blocking)
- **Format**: Small banner, easily dismissible
- **Example**: "Secure your crypto with XYZ hardware wallet"

#### ✅ **Dashboard Cards** (Optional)
- **When**: On main dashboard, below the fold
- **Format**: Native card, marked "Sponsored"
- **Example**: "Earn 5% APY on your ADA with ABC protocol"
- **User control**: Can be hidden in settings

#### ✅ **Asset Lists** (Sponsored Listings)
- **When**: In token/NFT lists
- **Format**: Clearly marked sponsored row
- **Example**: "Featured: New DEX token - learn more"
- **Limit**: Max 1 per 10 organic listings

#### ✅ **Chat Suggestions** (wAli Recommendations)
- **When**: User asks for help/recommendations
- **Format**: Natural language suggestion, marked as sponsored
- **Example**: "I recommend XYZ staking pool (sponsored partner)"

### Prohibited Placements

#### ❌ **During Critical Operations**
- No ads while signing transactions
- No ads during wallet recovery
- No ads in security/auth flows

#### ❌ **Intrusive Formats**
- No pop-ups or pop-unders
- No auto-play audio/video
- No full-screen takeovers
- No notifications mimicking system alerts

#### ❌ **Deceptive Placements**
- No ads disguised as wallet features
- No fake buttons or controls
- No impersonation of wAli brand

## Content Guidelines

### Allowed Content

#### ✅ **Crypto Services**
- Exchanges and trading platforms
- Staking pools and validators
- DeFi protocols (verified)
- NFT marketplaces
- Blockchain infrastructure
- Educational resources

#### ✅ **Related Services**
- Hardware wallets
- Security tools
- Tax software
- Portfolio trackers
- News and analytics

### Prohibited Content

#### ❌ **Scams & Fraud**
- Ponzi schemes and pyramid schemes
- Pump-and-dump schemes
- Fake giveaways ("Send 1 ADA, get 10 back")
- Impersonation of legitimate projects
- Phishing attempts

#### ❌ **High-Risk Financial**
- Unregistered securities
- Unlicensed financial advice
- Guaranteed returns claims
- Get-rich-quick schemes

#### ❌ **Inappropriate Content**
- Adult content
- Violence or hate speech
- Illegal goods or services
- Gambling (in restricted jurisdictions)
- Drugs or weapons

#### ❌ **Misleading Claims**
- False or unverifiable statistics
- Fake endorsements
- Manipulated images
- Comparison without evidence

## Privacy Standards

### Data Collection

#### What We DON'T Collect
- ❌ Personal identity (name, email, etc.)
- ❌ Wallet addresses or balances
- ❌ Transaction history
- ❌ Browsing history outside wAli
- ❌ Location (precise GPS)

#### What We MAY Collect (With Consent)
- ✅ Country/region (for geo-targeting)
- ✅ General interests (if user opts in)
- ✅ Contextual action (what user is doing in wAli)
- ✅ Ad impressions/clicks (anonymized)

### User Controls

Users can:
- ✅ Disable all ads (in settings)
- ✅ Opt out of targeting (contextual-only mode)
- ✅ Hide specific ad placements
- ✅ Report inappropriate ads
- ✅ Clear ad preferences anytime

### Do Not Track

If user has "Do Not Track" enabled:
- No ads shown (default behavior)
- No impression tracking
- No analytics collection
- User can override if desired

## Advertiser Requirements

### Verification

All advertisers must:
1. Complete business verification
2. Provide valid contact information
3. Accept terms of service
4. Submit ads for review

### Ad Specifications

#### Banner Ads
- **Dimensions**: 320x50 (mobile), 728x90 (desktop)
- **File size**: Max 150KB
- **Format**: PNG, JPG, WebP (no GIFs, no animation)
- **Copy length**: Max 60 characters

#### Native Ads
- **Title**: Max 40 characters
- **Description**: Max 120 characters
- **Logo**: 64x64px, max 20KB
- **Image** (optional): 1200x630px, max 200KB

#### Sponsored Listings
- **Title**: Max 30 characters
- **Description**: Max 80 characters
- **Logo**: 48x48px, max 15KB

### Review Process

1. **Submission**: Advertiser submits ad creative
2. **Automated Check**: Scanned for malware, prohibited content
3. **Manual Review**: wAli team reviews (1-2 business days)
4. **Approval/Rejection**: Feedback provided if rejected
5. **Go Live**: Ad enters rotation if approved

### Ongoing Monitoring

Ads are monitored for:
- User reports (reviewed within 24 hours)
- Performance (CTR, bounce rate)
- Compliance (random spot checks)
- Landing page quality (must remain safe/relevant)

## Pricing Models

### CPM (Cost Per Mille)
- **Rate**: $5-$15 per 1,000 impressions
- **Best for**: Brand awareness, broad reach
- **Minimum budget**: $100

### CPC (Cost Per Click)
- **Rate**: $0.50-$2.00 per click
- **Best for**: Traffic generation, conversions
- **Minimum budget**: $200

### Flat Rate
- **Rate**: Custom (based on placement, duration)
- **Best for**: Exclusive partnerships, events
- **Minimum budget**: $500

### Payment Methods
- ✅ Cardano (ADA) - Recommended
- ✅ Credit card (Stripe)

## Reporting & Analytics

Advertisers receive:
- **Impressions**: Total views
- **Clicks**: Total clicks
- **CTR**: Click-through rate
- **Spend**: Total amount spent
- **Frequency**: Avg impressions per user

Advertisers do NOT receive:
- ❌ User identity or wallet addresses
- ❌ Individual user behavior
- ❌ Personal data

## User Reporting

Users can report ads for:
- Scam or fraud
- Inappropriate content
- Misleading claims
- Technical issues (broken links)
- Privacy concerns

**How to report:**
1. Click "Report Ad" button
2. Select reason
3. Submit (optional: provide details)

**Response time:**
- Critical (scams): Within 2 hours
- High priority: Within 24 hours
- Standard: Within 3 business days

## Enforcement

### Violations

First violation:
- Warning issued
- Ad paused for review
- Advertiser contacted

Second violation:
- Ad rejected
- Account suspended (7 days)
- Refund (if applicable)

Third violation:
- Account permanently banned
- No refund
- Blacklisted from future advertising

### Appeal Process

Advertisers can appeal:
1. Submit appeal within 7 days
2. Provide evidence/corrections
3. wAli reviews (3-5 business days)
4. Final decision communicated
